The challenge

Northwind's analysts were stitching together CSV exports across five regions every Monday. Reporting lagged the business by 6+ days and key decisions were being made on stale data.

Approach

I built a Laravel ingestion service that streams from POS APIs into a normalized warehouse, with queued jobs handling reconciliation. The front-end uses Livewire for live filters and a custom OLAP cube for slice-and-dice in milliseconds.

Outcome

Reporting latency dropped from 6 days to 4 minutes. The team killed 47 manual spreadsheets and surfaced a leak in EU pricing that recovered roughly $340k in the first quarter.
